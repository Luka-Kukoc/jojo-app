# Tooth models

The 3D tooth models in this folder (`*.glb`, compressed with gltf-transform) are by the **University of Dundee, School of Dentistry**
(created by Emily McDougall, content expert Dr. Andrew Mason, digital 3D preparation Mark Roughtly),
created in ZBrush from CT data and published on Sketchfab under
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0** (author must be credited, commercial use allowed).

Collection: https://sketchfab.com/DundeeDental/collections/permanent-teeth-4c0d0548c40c463c8cdceb6e0d08df7f

Right-side teeth in the app are mirrored copies of the left-side originals.
